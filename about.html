About Our Department

Discover who we are and what we stand for.

Who We Are

The Department of Computing is committed to providing students with quality education and practical knowledge in the field of information technology and computing. We prepare students to become skilled professionals capable of contributing to the development of society through technology.

Our Vision

To become a leading department in computing education, technological innovation, research and professional development.

Our Mission

To provide quality computing education and develop competent graduates with the technical skills, creativity and problem-solving abilities needed in the modern digital world.

Our Core Values

  • Academic Excellence
  • Innovation and Creativity
  • Integrity and Professionalism
  • Teamwork and Collaboration
  • Continuous Learning
